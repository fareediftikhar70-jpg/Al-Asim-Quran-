Empowering The Next Generation

Our Youth Programs offer a safe, dynamic, and inspiring environment designed to nurture personal growth, moral values, and community leadership among young individuals.

Through structured mentorship, interactive workshops, and social activities, we help teenagers and young adults build confidence, develop practical life skills, and form lifelong friendships grounded in shared principles.

Key Pillars of Our Program

Structured Curriculum

Systematic learning modules covering essential religious sciences, ethics, and practical guidelines.

Certified Instructors

Learn directly from verified scholars and experienced educators dedicated to your success.

Flexible Learning

Options for both in-person sessions and online interactive classes to fit your busy schedule.

Community Impact

Designed to build strong character, foster unity, and encourage active community involvement.

Services Outcomes & Benefits

Participants will acquire tangible skills and experiences designed to set them up for personal and professional success:

  • Enhanced communication and public speaking skills.
  • Strong foundational understanding of community governance and service.
  • Certificate of Completion & Verified Volunteer Hours for College Applications.
  • Access to an exclusive alumni networking and career development hub.

Frequently Asked Questions

Our youth programs are split into two tracks: Junior Youth (Ages 12–15) and Senior Youth (Ages 16–21) to ensure age-appropriate activities and discussions.

Sessions take place every Saturday afternoon from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM at our main community center hall.

Most regular weekend programs are free of charge. Occasional outdoor trips or retreats may require a nominal fee to cover transportation and supplies.
